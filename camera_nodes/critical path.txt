CRITICAL PATH LOOP: (can only happen on one core/thread)

    - camera capture (main and low_res in camera memory)

    - low_res => ema diffs

    - diffs => motion boxes (thresholding, eroding, dilating, contour finding of blobs, conversion to bounding boxes)

    - motion boxes => patches (use box coordinates to snip out relevant parts from main in camera memory)

    - release camera mem

    - timing stuff

    - pack patches, low_res, and timing data [and diffs and bgs] into a dict called frame
    
    - push frame onto a processing queue


OTHER STEPS: (can happen on one or more other cores/threads)

    jpeg encode low_res [and diffs and bgs]

    use camera calibration to convert patch coords to 3D ray

    serialize everything to send

    send patches and jpeg-encoded stuff
